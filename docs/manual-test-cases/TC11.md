# TC-11: Full checkout

**Priority:** High | **Type:** Positive | **Preconditions:** User is logged in

| Step | Action | Expected Result |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Navigate to the home page and choose Combination pliers | Product page for Combination pliers gets opened |
| 2 | Add Combination pliers to cart via `Add to cart` button | Product gets added to the cart that's visible in the navigation bar next to `Sign in` button |
| 3 | Press the cart icon | Cart page gets opened |
| 4 | On the cart page, press `Proceed to checkout` | Website continues with checkout process |
| 5 | On the sign in checker section, press `Proceed to checkout` | Billing address section of the page loads |
| 6 | On the billing address section, fill in valid data | Payment section of the page loads |
| 7 | On the payment section, choose a payment method `Credit Card` and fill in valid data | Payment section shows that the payment was successful |
| 8 | On the payment section, press confirm | Payment section shows invoice number |

**Result:** PASS
